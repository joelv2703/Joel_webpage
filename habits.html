SIMPLE CHOICES, EVERY DAY

My healthy habits.

Six easy ways to take care of my body and mind.

HABIT 01

Eat balanced meals

I will eat different kinds of food, including fruits, vegetables, grains, and protein. A balanced meal gives me energy for school and play.

Try this: Try adding a fruit or vegetable to each meal.

HABIT 02

Drink water

Water helps my body work well. I will carry a water bottle and drink water during the day, especially after playing.

Try this: Keep a water bottle in your school bag.

HABIT 03

Move every day

Walking, cycling, dancing, and playing sports are fun ways to stay active. Moving helps me feel stronger and happier.

Try this: Choose an activity you enjoy.

HABIT 04

Get enough sleep

Sleep helps me rest and focus in class. I will follow a regular bedtime and put screens away before bed.

Try this: Make bedtime a calm part of your day.

HABIT 05

Keep clean

I will wash my hands with soap before eating and after using the bathroom. I will also brush my teeth in the morning and at night.

Try this: Small daily habits help keep germs away.

HABIT 06

Take care of my mind

I will take breaks, do things I enjoy, and talk to someone I trust when I feel worried. My feelings matter too.

Try this: Try reading, drawing, or a quiet break.

One small step is a great start.
